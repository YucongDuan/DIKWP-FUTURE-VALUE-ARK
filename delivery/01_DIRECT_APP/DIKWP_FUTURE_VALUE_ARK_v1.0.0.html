5. Method

Purchasing power and liquidity floors

Emergency funding need equals monthly essential spending multiplied by reserve months, plus known near-term spending. Cash and short-term sovereign instruments must meet that share of the portfolio. If the need exceeds 95% of assets, the tool flags the cash-flow structure for review.

Robust optimization across scenarios

Within the declared constraints, the search evaluates candidate allocations by scenario returns, the worst result, the weighted bottom quarter, weighted mean, dispersion, concentration, costs and turnover. A fixed seed supports replay; the search does not prove global optimality.

Why include a correlation-failure scenario?

Assets can fall together in a crisis, while custody and payment access may also fail. This scenario challenges apparent gains across every declared case; it does not predict a particular event.

Rebalancing discipline

Review target ranges rather than a single point. Consider new cash flow first, and reassess when ranges, living needs or risk limits change. Evaluate costs, taxes, product credit and market impact independently before trading.
