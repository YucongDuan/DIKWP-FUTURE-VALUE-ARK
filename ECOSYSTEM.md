# Connected DIKWP Public Research Ecosystem

This repository is one of thirteen complementary open research releases associated with the DIKWP programme. Each project remains independently runnable, reviewable and licensable; the links below expose how the releases fit together without claiming that any prototype has already achieved clinical, legal, financial, governmental or scientific validation.

## Shared engineering grammar

Across these releases, the recurring pattern is:

1. explicit D/I/K/W/P objects and transformation boundaries;
2. plural world or interpretation models instead of one hidden final authority;
3. evidence and provenance records that can be inspected and challenged;
4. named purpose, consent, role and authority boundaries before consequential action;
5. zero automatic real-world authority unless a separately authorized integration is supplied;
6. reversibility, appeal, recovery conditions and reality-contact feedback;
7. clear separation between a research prototype, a deployable tool and external validation.

## Evidence boundary

Cross-linking shows design continuity and makes comparison easier. It does not establish endorsement by an institution, clinical efficacy, investment performance, legal validity, standards conformity, consciousness, identity or authority. Evaluate each repository by its own code, tests, sources, limitations and license.
